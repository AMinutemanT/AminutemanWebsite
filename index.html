SEQUENCE · HOW AN ENGAGEMENT CLOSES

Six steps, and a human at the fifth.

A force does not lose an engagement because it lacked a sensor. It loses because the radar that held the track, the battery that could have serviced it and the commander who could have authorised the shot were on three different systems, procured in three different decades, speaking three different formats. The track existed. The magazine existed. The path between them did not.

  • Sensing on the left, effects on the right, one custody model in the middle. Any node on either side can be ours, the customer's, or a third party's. The grid is indifferent to which.
  • Aorizon — the sovereign foundation model, and the picture it holds. It reads sensor telemetry as a first-class modality, which is what lets it turn every sensor a force owns into one continuously maintained picture.
  • Talon — effector pairing and engagement management. Talon takes a target and finds the right effect for it across everything on the grid, under an authority chain that is explicit at every step.
  • Sentinel — installation and force protection, layer by layer. Sentinel holds the defensive picture for a base, a convoy or a formation and runs the layered response automatically under standing authority.

↳ The sensor that saw it is almost never the shooter that can reach it. The grid compresses the four steps either side of the human.

POSTURE · CONTESTED CONDITIONS

Built for the link going down.

Contested electromagnetic conditions are the design case, handled in the first release and not deferred to a later one. Jamming, GNSS denial and loss of the rear link are the design case, worked in the first release. A system that needs a datacentre to close a kill chain is not one a force can fight on.

  • Five layers. A customer can adopt the lower ones without the upper ones, which is usually how a first deployment starts.
  • Edge autonomy that holds through disconnection, under human authority. Flight control, datalink and sensing that hold through denial and jamming.
  • Engagement authority is enforced in the software rather than asserted in a briefing, and every engagement carries an identity, a timestamp and an authority reference.
  • Valley reaches a customer as one of three deployable products. They read and write the same grid state, so a force can start with one and add the others without a second integration.

↳ A machine may propose; a human commits.
